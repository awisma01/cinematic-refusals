الفكرةThe argument

Mapping as method, not illustration

01

Checkpoints are not passive infrastructure. They are architectures of total surveillance: elevated cameras, panoptic visibility, continuous documentation.

02

Filmmakers document them differently. Jacir, Nabulsi, and Masri make counter-cartographies, cinematic maps that refuse surveillance’s spatial and temporal logic.

03

So the map has to make the claim itself. Counter-mapping works in the same register as the surveillance it answers: location, data, and visualization, used against the grain.

ليش اخترت هيكDecisions

Choices that shaped the archive

Glossary before bibliography

Public readers need the vocabulary (counter-cartography, sumud, ellipsis, repertoire) to enter the analysis.

Palestinian dialect, not MSA

Palestinians are the main audience. It slows the work, and it is the point.

Clips embedded, not hosted

Copyright, and an archive under erasure: many films are out of circulation or destroyed.

Refusing the basemap’s names

Available basemaps use Hebrew place names. Restoring Palestinian names is the next priority.

Seed grant: 3 of 6 films analyzed · map rebuilt in Mapbox · community submissions and Arabic interface added beyond the plan
