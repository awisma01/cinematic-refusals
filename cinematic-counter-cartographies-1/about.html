About

Learn more about the Cinematic Counter-Cartographies project, its goals, and the contributors behind it.

Project Goals

The Cinematic Counter-Cartographies project aims to explore the intersections of cinema, geography, and social justice. Through various analyses and visualizations, we seek to highlight underrepresented narratives and challenge dominant representations in film.

Contributors

This project is a collaborative effort involving filmmakers, scholars, and activists dedicated to promoting critical discourse around cinematic representations.
